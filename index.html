About The Game

The Ancient Race, Reborn Digital

Rune Race takes the beloved Vietnamese Cá Ngựa (Horse Racing / Ludo) game and transforms it into a modern online multiplayer experience packed with depth, drama, and delicious strategy.

While the classic game relies purely on dice rolls, Rune Race layers in a strategic Rune card system, simultaneous card placement, and a groundbreaking disguise mechanic that turns every move into psychological warfare.

Accessible enough for the whole family, deep enough for competitive minds — Rune Race is the party game that never plays the same way twice.

Core Mechanics

The Rune System

Three families of cards shape every match. Know them, wield them, and never reveal your true hand.

🕊️
Support Rune
Escape Stable

Immediately release a piece from your stable onto the board — no dice roll required. Perfect for getting into the race when fortune refuses to cooperate.

⚡
Support Rune
Double Steps

Your chosen piece moves double the distance of your dice roll this turn. Close the gap on leaders or secure a last-second victory with one dramatic surge.

🛡️
Support Rune
Shield

Protect one of your pieces from the next Trap card that targets it. Your opponent played a Freeze? Not today. The Shield absorbs it entirely.

❄️
Trap Rune
Freeze

Lock a targeted opponent's piece in place for one full round. They can't move it, can't protect it — they can only watch and hope you don't capitalize.

🏠
Trap Rune
Return Home

Send a target piece all the way back to its stable. Brutal when used against a piece inches from the finish line. The most feared card in any match.

⬅️
Trap Rune
Move Backward

Force a target piece to retreat a set number of squares. A precision strike — surgical when timed correctly, devastating when it knocks someone off a safe tile.

🔄
Special Rune
Position Swap

Instantly swap positions between any two pieces on the board — yours and an opponent's, or two opponents' pieces. The most chaotic and dramatic card in the deck.

🎭
Special Rune · Signature
Disguise

When playing a Trap card, you may disguise your identity as another player. The victim believes someone else attacked them — chaos, betrayal, and broken alliances ensue. The heart of Rune Race's psychological warfare. Refuse to use Disguise for 5 turns? Earn a free Support Rune as your reward for playing it straight.

How It Works

Deception Is A Strategy

When you play a Trap card, you have the option to disguise yourself as another player. The targeted player sees a different attacker — suspicion falls on the wrong person.

Every table conversation, every accusation, every alliance becomes suspect. Who really sent that Freeze card? The tension is delicious.
