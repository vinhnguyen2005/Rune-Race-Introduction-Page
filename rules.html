What is Rune Race?
Game Overview
Rune Race — MVP Rulebook
Online Multiplayer · 4 Players · Inspired by Cá Ngựa

Rune Race is built on the foundation of Vietnam's beloved Cá Ngựa (Ludo) board game, extended with a strategic Rune card layer. The Rune system does not replace the base rules of Cá Ngựa — it adds an additional layer of tactics and psychology on top of it.

🎯
Core Document ScopeThis rulebook covers: the new turn loop with Rune cards, the 11 official Rune types, secret marker placement, the simultaneous-play phase, the Disguise system, and step-by-step movement resolution.
🏛️
Based on Cá Ngựa

The traditional Vietnamese Ludo rules remain the base. Rune cards create a second layer of strategy.

🌐
4 Players Online

Real-time online multiplayer via room codes. All actions are server-validated for fairness.

🎭
Deception Layer

The Disguise system lets players frame opponents, hide their identity, and create social drama.

Philosophy
Design Principles

Inherit Traditional Rules

Rune Race does not replace the existing Cá Ngựa base rules in the MVP. The Rune system creates an additional tactical and psychological layer.

Intentional Public Information

Players see marker positions and display identities, but never the Rune type or the real placer's identity.

Bidirectional Effects

Any piece that lands on or passes through a marker can be helped or harmed — including a piece owned by the marker's real placer.

Controlled Chaos

All players may place cards simultaneously in the same phase. Position conflicts are resolved by the server in order of receipt.

Key Terms
Official Terminology
Normal TurnA turn beginning with the draw-card step. This is the unit used to count most time limits in the game.
Bonus Turn (rolled 6)A sub-turn that only repeats from the dice roll step. Does NOT open a new draw phase or simultaneous placement phase; it does NOT count as a new timer round.
Hand ArrayUp to 10 active (non-expired) cards held by one player, displayed bottom-left of the screen.
MarkerA secret location indicator created when a card is placed on the shared path. The marker never reveals the Rune type.
Real PlacerThe player who actually placed the marker. Never revealed to other players — not on trigger, not on expiry, not at game end.
Display IdentityThe player whose avatar and color appear on the marker. May be the real placer or a disguised player.
Pass-Through MarkerA marker that triggers the moment a piece passes over it, even if the piece does not stop on that cell.
Exact-Stop MarkerA marker that only triggers if movement ends exactly on the marker's cell.
Shared PathCells usable by multiple players. Only these cells may hold markers.
How a Turn Works
The Turn Loop
⚠️
Two Types of TurnsNormal Turns and Bonus Turns (from rolling 6) follow different rules. See the table below for what each allows.

Normal Turn — 7 Steps

1

Open Normal Turn

Process any expired cards in the active player's hand array before drawing or placing.

2

Draw Cards

The active player may draw 0 or more random Rune cards, as long as their personal draw total hasn't hit 25 and their hand hasn't hit 10.

3

Simultaneous Placement Phase

All players holding valid cards may place any number of them on legal cells. Players may also hold back cards for later phases.

4

Roll Dice

Only the active player rolls.

5

Select Piece

Choose a legal piece following traditional Cá Ngựa rules and any active status effects.

6

Move Step by Step

The piece moves one cell at a time. The server resolves each cell and any markers on the path (see Movement Rules).

7

End Turn

Update status counters, marker lifetimes, and any related effects.

Hand Management
Drawing Cards & Hand Array
🃏
25-Card Personal Limit

Each player may draw at most 25 random Rune cards total per game. This is a per-player cap, not a shared pool. Bonus-turn draws are never allowed.

🗂️
Hand Array: Max 10 Cards

Each player holds up to 10 active (non-expired) cards at once, shown bottom-left on screen. When the array is full, no further drawing is possible. Each card displays its remaining rounds.

Card Placement
Placing Markers
Simultaneous Placement Phase
All players act at the same moment

The placement phase opens after the active player draws cards, and before they roll. All players holding valid cards may participate — not just the active player. There is no limit on how many cards a player may place in one phase.

Signature Feature
The Disguise System
🎭
How Disguise Works

When placing any marker (Support, Trap, or Special), the real placer selects a display identity from among active players. Others see that identity's avatar and color on the marker — never the true placer.

🔒
Permanently Secret

The real placer's identity is NEVER revealed — not when the marker triggers, not when it expires, not at game end. This is by design.

Disguise Rules

Per-Card Rules
Individual Card Details
➕

Advance 2 / 3 / 4 Steps Support

Marker disappears on trigger. Steps are added to current forward movement. If the piece is being pushed backward, the backward remainder is cancelled and the piece moves forward exactly the card's amount. Further markers along the bonus steps still resolve normally.

⬅️

Backward 3 / 4 / 5 Steps Trap

Marker disappears on trigger. If moving forward, remaining forward steps are cancelled and piece retreats the card's amount. If already moving backward, steps are added together. All markers encountered during the retreat still resolve. Shield blocks and consumes this trap.

🛡️

Shield Support

Marker disappears on trigger. A piece can hold at most ONE Shield layer — hitting a second Shield marker while already shielded discards the marker but grants no extra protection. Shield lasts indefinitely until it absorbs one Backward, Freeze, or Send Home trap — or the piece is kicked to stable. Shield does NOT block Swap.

❄️

Freeze Trap

Triggers on pass-through (no Shield required). Piece stops on the marker cell; all remaining steps discarded. The piece cannot be selected for 2 of the owner's normal turns. Bonus turns (rolled 6) do NOT count down the Freeze timer and the frozen piece may not be selected during them. Being kicked to stable by another piece clears Freeze immediately.

🏡

Send Home Trap · Exact Stop

Only triggers when movement ends exactly on the marker cell. Piece is immediately returned to its stable. Shield absorbs this — marker disappears and Shield layer is consumed. The most devastating card when triggered near the finish line.

🏠

Escape Stable Support · Exact Stop

Only triggers when movement ends exactly on the marker cell. Marker disappears regardless of effect. If the triggering piece's owner has pieces in the stable, one is moved to the starting cell per traditional Cá Ngựa rules. If the starting cell holds an opponent's piece, standard kick-to-stable rules apply. If stable is empty, no extra effect. The triggering piece keeps its current position.

🔄

Position Swap Special · Exact Stop

Only triggers when movement ends exactly on the marker cell. Marker disappears regardless. The controlling player selects a valid piece belonging to the display identity on the shared path to swap with. Ineligible: pieces in stable, at finish, or on private home stretch. If the display identity is the same as the piece's owner — or has no valid piece — no swap occurs. Shield does NOT block Swap.

When Display Identity Leaves the Game
Markers don't disappear — they switch to global round counting

If the display identity quits or finishes all their pieces after a marker is placed, the marker persists. TTL counting switches to whole-table rounds. The remaining TTL value carries over unchanged — one completed table round reduces it by one.

Freeze Status Details

❄️
How Freeze is counted: If piece A (owned by Player A) is Frozen during Player B's turn, Player A's next 2 normal turns are blocked for that piece. After Player A completes their second blocked normal turn, Freeze clears. If the piece is kicked to stable before that, Freeze clears immediately. Bonus turns never count toward clearing Freeze.

Common Questions
Frequently Asked Questions
Can I draw cards on a bonus turn (rolled 6)?

No. A bonus turn only repeats the dice roll, piece selection, and movement steps. The draw-cards step and simultaneous placement phase are not opened again during a bonus turn.

Does the real placer's identity ever get revealed?

Never. The real placer is permanently hidden — not when a marker triggers, not when it expires naturally, and not when the game ends. This is a core design principle of the Disguise system.

Can my own piece trigger a marker you placed?

Yes. Any piece that passes through or stops on a marker cell triggers it — regardless of who the real placer is. This is the "bidirectional effects" design principle: placement decisions carry real risk for the placer too.

What happens if two players try to place on the same cell?

The server accepts whichever request arrived first. The later request fails — but the card is NOT consumed. It stays in the player's hand array and can be placed on a different cell in a future phase.

Does Freeze count down during bonus turns?

No. The Freeze counter only decreases on the affected piece owner's normal turns. Bonus turns from rolling 6 do not count down the Freeze and do not allow the frozen piece to be selected.

Does Shield block Swap (Position Swap)?

No. Shield only blocks the three trap categories: Backward, Freeze, and Send Home. It does NOT protect against the Swap special card.

Do bonus reward cards count toward my 25-draw limit?

No. The free Support Rune earned after 5 consecutive honest turns is a reward, not a draw. It does not consume any of your 25-draw personal quota.

What happens to a marker if its display identity quits mid-game?

The marker stays on the board. Its TTL countdown switches from "display identity's turns" to "whole-table rounds." The remaining TTL value is preserved exactly as-is — one completed table round reduces it by one until the marker expires naturally.

Can I stack multiple Shield layers on one piece?

No. A piece can hold at most one Shield layer. If a shielded piece passes through another Shield marker, the marker disappears but no extra protection is granted.

What counts as an "honest turn" for the honesty streak?

A turn is honest if and only if: (1) the player placed at least one marker that phase, AND (2) every marker placed in that phase displays the player's own identity. Turns with no placement at all neither advance nor reset the counter.
